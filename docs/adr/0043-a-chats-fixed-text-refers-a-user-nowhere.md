# A chat's fixed text refers a user nowhere: General states what it does not do and declines, and every text a user reads says Legal, never Research

Four release texts sent a user to "Research": General's preset description ("For anything you will rely on, use Research.") and its system prompt's citation rule ([06] items 4–5), the search-confirmation dialog's last sentence ([06] item 15, §15), and the citation stamp's second sentence ([06] item 16, §15); §21's practice rule, carried by the release-note template, said "there is no Research to check it in" ([33]). ADR-0038 then made Legal the chat's display name, so every referral pointed at a name no user will see, and slice-1 ticket 70's users-seat proof showed every doctrine answer ending by sending the reader to Research. The ruling is that a chat's fixed text refers a user nowhere: General's texts state what it does not do and decline — the description ends at "and it verifies nothing", the citation rule says the model has not verified a citation and, asked to verify one, that it cannot, the dialog ends at "Do not paste client or case material here.", and the stamp is the one-sentence label "General does not verify citations." — and "Legal" supersedes "Research" in every text a user reads, the practice rule reading "until go-live there is no Legal chat to check it in" and the pre-launch coverage line "General only; the Legal chat arrives at go-live on SCOTUS and the Sixth Circuit." Research stays the name of the service, the route, the suites, and the domain term (ADR-0038 stands; its "release text written by slice 4's preset ticket" is written under this rule). A text that sends a user to another surface moves whenever that surface's name or arrival moves; a text that states its own chat's limit does not. CSA ruling at slice-1 ticket 18's discovery, 2026-09-17, recorded on tickets 18 and 83.

**Rejected:** rewording each referral to "the Legal chat" — before go-live it refers a user to a chat that does not exist, and the text moves again when it arrives; leaving "Research" until the Legal chat's record lands — a user-facing name no user will ever see, in front of every user from the first user-facing release.

**Consequences:** the four texts move in slice-1 ticket 83, the stamp's Function to version 2; the release-note template's coverage line and practice rule, with `tests/test_release_notes.py`'s constant, take the Legal wording, so the `v0.2.0` note (slice-1 ticket 18) is written from it; the General smoke set's `identity-01`, `citation-01`, and `verify-01` are superseded by cases that hold General to naming no other chat; §15's fixed texts and §21's rule wording are superseded by dated comments on greenfield [06] and [33], the spec not edited (slice-1 ticket 29's pattern); the glossary's *Privilege reminder*, *Pre-launch release*, and *Legal* entries move (this commit); the Legal chat's own texts, at standing ticket 20's record, are written under the same rule. ADR-0020 (the stamp is a label, never a verdict) and ADR-0028 (fixed product text, no site value) are unchanged in kind; no ADR is superseded.
