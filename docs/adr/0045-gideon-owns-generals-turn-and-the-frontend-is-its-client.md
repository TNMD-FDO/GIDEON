# GIDEON owns General's turn, as it owns the Legal chat's: General's own Chat Completions service, the guardrail one shared module, the frontend a client

The two chats (ADR-0038) were specified with two postures. The Legal chat's turn is GIDEON's from the start: §13.1's `POST /turn` runs the whole turn, the Pipe is transport, and the service is the only caller of `gideon-generator` on Research's behalf. General's is the frontend's: Open WebUI connects straight to the engine over Chat Completions (§5.2), and everything GIDEON does to a General turn runs inside the frontend as two Functions — the arithmetic guardrail, a global Filter holding the judge, the lag window, the reasoning withholding, and the trip's kept event (§16, ADR-0006, ADR-0039), and the citation stamp, an outlet Filter on General's record (§15). The guardrail reaches that far only by knowing the frontend's internals, and each cycle on it has paid for that: its per-request state rides `__metadata__`, a trip ends the engine's generation by cancelling the frontend's own chat task, which exists on the browser path alone, and the kept event is written from inside the frontend's container under a secret the frontend must mount. The decision is that **GIDEON owns General's turn too, and the frontend is a client of it**: (a) General's turn moves into a GIDEON service of its own in `gideon-api`, separate from `/turn`, which the frontend reaches as its Chat Completions connection — the connection's base URL becomes the service's, the service speaks the OpenAI-compatible API on both sides, and it is the only caller of `gideon-generator` on General's behalf: it calls the engine, runs the guardrail over the stream, appends the stamp, and returns the result; (b) the judge — the pattern families, the lag window, the withholding, the refusals, and the kept event's row with its write under the audit role — is one `gideon` module, called by General's service now and by `/turn` from its first ticket on the stream each owns, the stamp living in General's service alone; (c) the service judges every completion it serves, a stream through the window and a non-streamed completion whole, the frontend's task calls included, so the session refusal retires, and the frontend's user-info headers are rendered on in their plain form and read for the kept event's `source` word alone, never logged; (d) the branch refusal stays in the frontend as an inlet-only global Function, the one GIDEON Function left, because a preset's turn reaches the connection under its base's id and the base row cannot lose its public-read grant without General answering "Model not found", so neither the wire nor an access grant can tell the hidden base from General; (e) the outlet's whole-message replacement is a requirement nowhere — a trip leaves the released prefix and the refusal on every path, the lag window's invariant being that the released prefix never held a matched span; (f) the build is its own effort, `general-turn`, on patch tags with the cutover before `v0.3.0`, free of any window since no user reaches the box before go-live (ADR-0044), slice 2's `guardrails` and `general-smoke` tickets waiting on the cutover alone; (g) the global guardrail Filter retires at that cutover. The direction this serves is **a replaceable frontend**: Open WebUI stays the frontend, unforked (ADR-0001), and what GIDEON takes from it is a written contract — [`docs/frontend-contract.md`](../frontend-contract.md) — that shrinks a layer at a time, when a cycle is already there and never as a project of its own; this decision moves the first layer. The maintainer's ruling at standing ticket 78's triage and the word after it, Sept 2026.
