# Two chats, General and Legal: Research, Review, and Draft are what the Legal chat does, in that order, never branches of their own; Transcribe is the sibling service

The spec's §0 names five user-facing branches — General and Research in v1, Review, Transcribe, and Draft as fog — each a preset over the generator and a selectable model in the frontend, and §22.4 has the three fog branches graduate after `v1.0.0` in the order Review → Transcribe → Draft, each as its own effort. The product's shape is ruled to be two chats and no more: **General**, the public-chatbot replacement whose user text may leave the box as search, and **Legal**, the chat for anything about a case and anything confidential, whose text never leaves the box. Research, Review, and Draft are what the Legal chat does, delivered in that order — Research first (the corpus, the verification gate, go-live at `v0.5.0`), Review second (the matter's documents, discovery, transcripts, and exhibits, with the extraction that reads them), Draft third — and each is still its own effort with its own map or plan, landing as something the Legal chat does (a route, an ingestion feature, a rendering shape), never as a preset a user selects. The spec had already made most of this fold: every Research turn queries the corpus and every attached matter knowledge base and session upload, attachment being the routing, with a `matter_facts` route and a `drafting` route (§11.4); every user upload is the `matter` ingestion profile with page and Bates anchors and citation extraction (§17.2), arriving at Ingestion GA (`v0.7.0`, §22.1) before `v1.0.0`; and what §17.2 and §22.4 defer to "Review" — the vision-model fallback for low-confidence scans, entity and date extraction, the claim and exhaustion matrix, the exhibit citation form — are worker outputs and rendering shapes a chat consumes. Transcribe is not a GIDEON branch: transcription is Gideon Transcribe, the office's other project on the same box in its own repository (`docs/box-ledger.md`), and a transcript reaches the Legal chat as a matter upload, for which the transcript anchor kinds §22.4 reserves (`tr_page`, `tr_line`) stay reserved. The build order does not change: §22.3's sequence stands, and §22.4's order loses only Transcribe. "Research" stays the name of the service, the route, and the domain term; "Legal" is the chat's display name, a release text written by slice 4's preset ticket. CSA ruling in a tracker session following slice-1 ticket 27's triage, Sept 2026.

**Rejected:** a preset per branch, Review and Draft as selectable models beside Research — a user would have to know which chat holds the case's documents, when the boundary that matters to a user is the one between the internet and the box, and the reserved seats, the gate, and the provenance line already carry corpus and matter hits through one turn; folding Review's pipeline features forward into v1 — the sequence is normative, the no-matter-documents rule before Ingestion GA exists because ADR-0008's enforcement below the UI is not built until slice 6, and Review's triggers (the fallback rate on low-confidence scans, extraction) cannot be measured before real discovery is in the stores; a Legal chat before Research ships — there is nothing for it to do; Transcribe as a GIDEON branch — the sibling project exists and runs.

**Consequences:** the glossary's Branch entry names two chats, its Spine entry loses Transcribe, and it gains Legal and Gideon Transcribe (this commit); §0's branch sentence and §22.4's fog order are superseded by a comment on greenfield [24], the spec not edited (slice-1 ticket 29's pattern); standing ticket 46's selector (slice-1's until its triage) holds two entries, General first, and only ever two; standing ticket 20's "Research preset" (slice-1's until its triage) is the Legal chat's record, its File Upload interval unchanged and the lift standing ticket 75's; one record means one capability set for every use of the Legal chat, uploads on or off for research and review alike; the go-live note's coverage line and practice rule carry the caution that a chat called Legal draws legal questions the corpus does not yet cover before tranches 2–6, which abstain where General would answer from the web; a later Review or Draft effort names the Legal chat's record and route it lands on, never a new preset; an integration with Gideon Transcribe — a transcript into a matter knowledge base — is a box-ledger line when it is designed, none today. No ADR is superseded: ADR-0009's "every branch is a preset over the one generator" is satisfied by two.
