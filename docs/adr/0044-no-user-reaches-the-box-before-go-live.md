# No user reaches the box before go-live

Go-live is the `v0.5.0` release at which the Legal chat is offered to end users; until then no end user reaches the box. The `v0.2.0` tag is therefore a pre-launch release carrying General, its release note, and its proof, shipped to the maintainer and Transcribe's operator alone; the supervisor was informed on 2026-09-17. From go-live, the maintenance-window rule for an apply that recreates a service users see, the quiet-window rule beside users, the supervisor's working-day notice, the announced window, the CSAs' sent note, and the human read at patch and hotfix tags bind; the release note for every tag and the human read at minor tags remain at `v0.2.0`. This supersedes §21's sentence **General ships first, to all ~50 users, as a pre-launch release** — including its statement that it carries the deadline guardrail and citation stamp and that no pilot mechanism exists — and re-keys §21's maintenance-window, communications, and practice-rule consequences to go-live; it supersedes §22's inherited-rules sentence that says **after General ships, every user-facing tag deploys in an announced maintenance window**; §22.1's slice-1 row's **General to all ~50 users**; §22.3's **slice 1 (General to all; quiet windows begin)**; and the handoff note's order line **Slice 0 Platform (`v0.1.0`) → 1 General pre-launch (`v0.2.0`, all users)** and its sequencing rule **every user-facing tag after General deploys in an announced maintenance window**. It amends ADR-0036's **from `v0.2.0`** rule for a patch or hotfix touching the user-text paths — minor tags still take the human read from `v0.2.0`, while patch and hotfix tags take it from go-live — ADR-0037's premise that **before `v0.2.0` puts General in front of the office's users, with their chats on the box** — user chats and case data arrive at go-live, with standing ticket 76 owning the narrowing — and ADR-0040's consequence that the box's image-store move precedes `v0.2.0` because **after it every Docker restart is a user-facing window** — a restart is user-facing from go-live. The ledger's §7 hours still govern bulk engine load for Transcribe's client; this ruling changes when the user-facing rules begin, not those hours.

**Rejected:** putting General in front of every user now while Legal is three slices away — the practice rule would have nothing to check in and live chats would sit beside development sessions before standing ticket 76 is ruled; a pilot group — §21 provides no pilot mechanism; skipping `v0.2.0` — every slice ends in its reserved tagged release and the acceptance record is the minor tag's.

**Consequences:** the pre-launch glossary entry says that General is carried with no end user on the box and retains the practice rule; the dated comments on greenfield [24] and [33] record the spec supersession without editing the spec; the living user-facing rules are re-keyed to go-live, while the release-note floor and minor-tag read remain at `v0.2.0`; standing ticket 76 owns the user-data narrowing at go-live. No site key or new term is introduced.
