# Relevance selects, reservations guarantee, hierarchy orders, and abstention is code: the final set is chosen by reranker score alone, guaranteed by reserved seats rather than fusion weights, ordered by court-hierarchy weight, and a turn with no surviving hit abstains without calling the generator

GIDEON's retrieval layer fuses every leg of a Research turn with plain Reciprocal Rank Fusion (k = 60, every leg an equal voice, in GIDEON code), and the guarantees the design needs — exact objects never dropped, controlling authority always present, the matter leg keeping its share — are **reserved seats** in the candidate pool and in the final set, never weights on the fusion. Two rerankers (with independent off switches, the off/off order being the eval baseline) score relevance; the final set is **selected** by that raw score above an abstention floor, then **ordered** by score × authority-tier weight so controlling authority is presented first and persuasive last. Hits under the floor never reach the generator; when none survive, the turn **abstains in deterministic code** with a fixed notice, the generator is not called, and the floor is a release artifact tuned on the in-domain judgment set so that the eval's unanswerable questions abstain 100 % of the time, whatever false-refusal rate that costs at first. Greenfield-spec ticket 15, Aug 2026; evidence in `docs/research/retrieval-fusion-collapse-and-score-floors.md` (branch `research/retrieval-fusion-collapse-and-score-floors`).

**Rejected:** per-leg fusion weights or per-leg quotas — a guarantee is legible to a CSA and a weight is a knob nobody can explain, and quotas waste seats on turns that don't need them; score fusion — BM25 and cosine share no scale and RRF needs no calibration (learned fusion is a revisit once judgments exist); fusing the dense legs inside Qdrant — it ships k = 2 and would pre-fuse four dense voices into one against several lexical voices; a score-based pre-rerank filter — leg scores aren't comparable and a floor there drops the modest-scoring `cites`-term hit that is the one opinion that matters; hierarchy weight applied to *selection*, in fusion or after reranking — on a circuit-split question the out-of-circuit cases are the answer and a 0.35 discount on selection cuts the on-point Ninth Circuit case for a marginal home one (labels-only tier-then-score ordering is the recorded fallback if E23 prefers it); MMR — it penalises the second on-point chunk of the controlling case for resembling the first, so diversity is a per-document cap with the lookup route exempt; CRAG's loop and its strip-level refinement — no model in the retrieval loop on the shared GPU (ADR-0016), and recomposed text breaks the offsets contract (ADR-0013); asking the generator to decline when support is thin — that is asking a model not to use its memory, the one thing the accuracy layer exists to not trust; a site-editable floor or weight table — a configuration the eval triple never judged, and a typo silently disables the safety property; dynamic degradation under load — the same question would return different evidence at different hours and eval runs would not reproduce.

**Consequences:** every retrieval response carries a support level (`none | thin | ok`) beside its provenance, and the Pipe has a code path that ends a turn without a generator call; every hit carries an authority tier computed from court, precedential status (unknown treated as unpublished, never promoted), and the site's `jurisdiction`, and "persuasive" is never omitted from the label; the floor's shipped value is a placeholder replaced before go-live by judgment-set tuning, and the first release may over-refuse; the in-domain judgment set gates every depth and the floor, so it is a go-live prerequisite, not a nicety; the eval reports the no-reranker and no-abstention baselines on every full run; no site key exists for any ranking or abstention figure; results are never paginated in v1.
