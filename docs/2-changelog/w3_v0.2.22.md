## Changes

This release implements general-turn ticket 06 under the plan `docs/1-plans/service-judges-every-completion.plan.md`, spec §16, §17.1 and §19.4, ADR-0045 rulings (a), (c) and (e), and `docs/frontend-contract.md` §2 rows 2 and 7. Since `v0.2.15` the relay handed the engine's bytes to the caller unparsed; it now judges them. The judge is `gideon/guardrail.py`, called as it stands — not one line of it changed — and the chunk mechanics are written new against the engine's stream.

- **Event reassembly is its own module** (`gideon/api/sse.py`, new): `EventReassembler.feed` accumulates raw chunks and returns whole events. No research note establishes that one `aiter_raw()` chunk is one event, or even a whole character, so nothing downstream assumes it. The incomplete-event buffer carries a bound, and an overrun is the error path rather than unbounded growth.
- **The judgement is pure** (`gideon/api/judged.py`, new): `stream_state_from_body` builds the guardrail's `StreamState` from the request's own `messages` — a body with none readable still judges, on a context of its own — `StreamMechanics` carries the stream's chunk handling, and `judge_completion` the whole body's. The module reads the judge and never edits it: `StreamState`, `StreamCheck.append` (text in, released text out), `message_context`, `REFUSAL_BY_FAMILY`, `REFUSAL_SEPARATOR`, `record_trip`.
- **Reasoning never leaves the service.** A delta under `reasoning`, `reasoning_content`, or `thinking` is withheld and nothing is emitted in its place, so reasoning shows as silence after the role chunk. A relayed choice carries `index` and `finish_reason` with a delta of `role`, `content`, and `tool_calls` alone; `logprobs` and the engine's own extras are dropped, because the door reads `content`, `reasoning`, and `[DONE]` and nothing else does.
- **On a trip the engine's connection closes first, then the refusal is sent** (`gideon/api/relay.py:155`). The order is the point. A Filter had no handle on the upstream: the browser path cancelled the frontend's own chat task and the direct path could not stop the engine at all — `v0.1.18` measured a refusal shown near 25 s over a generation that ran 269.82 s. A service is the engine's client, so one await ends the generation on every path, and doing it before the several ASGI sends that carry the refusal is what keeps the wasted generation bounded by a connection teardown.
- **A tripped stream ends as the engine ends any stream**: the released prefix, the module's separator, the family's refusal, a finish chunk under `stop` (the maintainer's ruling at discovery), then `[DONE]`. The pinned frontend stores and paints what the stream carried and never reads `finish_reason` (contract §2 row 2), so a novel end shape would be a shape nothing is known to store. A trip's `created` and `id` are the engine's envelope, never minted; with no envelope seen, none is sent.
- **The four unfinished ends each settle the held tail** before what follows: the end marker with no finish chunk, a clean end without one, a transport failure, and the engine's own `error` event. On the transport path the tail rides out **before** the error events, so a failure never overtakes text the window legitimately held — and if that tail itself trips, the error events are not sent and the refusal ends the stream instead. A usage chunk after the finish chunk passes.
- **A whole completion is judged whole and answered whole**, the refusal in place of a tripped answer, reasoning stripped from the message, `null` content and several choices each carried. The frontend's task calls — a chat's title and tags, the search's queries — arrive as whole completions on this connection and take the same path with no branch of their own (contract §2 row 7).
- **An unjudged completion is never relayed.** A body the judge cannot read, an event past the buffer's bound, or a mechanics failure becomes an OpenAI-shaped error under the code `completion_unjudged`, content-free. Every failure path names its cause by exception class alone on `gideon.api.relay` — the code review's one finding, which asked that the class not be discarded, addressed at nine sites and pinned by `tests/test_api_completions.py:824-860`.
- **The import tripwire gained a deferred-only allowance.** `API_SOURCES` now declares the shared judge, and that module imports its Postgres driver inside `write_trip_row` — a name the image does not carry until ticket 07. `tests/test_service_import_boundary.py` admits that one name inside a function and refuses it at module level, with a planted violation each side. Ticket 07 empties the allowance when the image carries the driver.
- **Tests** (`tests/test_api_sse.py` and `tests/test_api_judged.py`, new; four modules changed; 41 new tests, 222 passing): reassembly with an event split at every byte offset, a multi-byte character split, several events in one chunk, CRLF framing, a comment dropped, and the bound. The Function's split-offset sweeps ported — every seed positive and control through the mechanics at granularities of one, seven, and word pieces, the large sweeps marked slow — with the invariant that **no released prefix ever trips under `judge_rendered`**, every positive tripping once with its family's refusal and controls within `len(controls) // 20`. The separator present after a released prefix and absent before one, a literal the Function's tests never asserted. Reasoning withheld under all three names and a fourth no list holds. At the ASGI and socket tiers, the stub's close observed before the refusal is sent, and over real sockets the stub's connection ending while the client has yet to read the refusal.

The 0.248 s fall is the direct evidence that the close ends the generation; the uncancelled figure is the scale of generation a full answer costs, which is what the close avoids paying.
