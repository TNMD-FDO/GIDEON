## Changes

This release implements general-turn ticket 09 under the plan `docs/1-plans/cutover.plan.md`: ADR-0045 made true on the box. The frontend's one OpenAI-compatible connection now names `gideon-api` instead of `gideon-generator`, the arithmetic guardrail's and the citation stamp's Functions are deleted, and everything built against them — their files, their plumbing tests, the two statement-equality checks, the session refusal and its two probe rows, the classifiers' reading of the outlet's replaced shape — is deleted or narrowed in the same cycle. 82 files, 1,932 insertions against 7,195 deletions. The judge's policy — the patterns, the window, the refusals — moves nowhere and changes nowhere.

- **The render, in one shape.** `owui_environment` renders `OPENAI_API_BASE_URLS` from `api_base_url()`, the frontend's `NO_PROXY` names `API_SERVICE_NAME`, and the env artifact's secret list carries `gideon_api_key` where it carried `engine_api_key`; `TASK_MODEL_EXTERNAL` stays the generator's served name, since the service passes the engine's `GET /v1/models` listing through. `ENABLE_FORWARD_USER_INFO_HEADERS` renders `"true"` beside the base URL, under the same `connected` condition, so a no-GPU host with no service forwards nothing.
