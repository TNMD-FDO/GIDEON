## Changes

- **The judge's prefix mode** (`compose/open-webui/functions/arithmetic_guardrail.py`; §16, ADR-0006): `judge_text` learns it is judging a prefix of a text still being generated and how much of it was decided at the last call (`since`): a hit ending at or before `since` is skipped, a hit ending within the restatement look-ahead of the end is undecided and skipped, and an introduced quotation of the user's words still open at the end is paired provisionally while the end lies short of the quotation scan's forward reach. In prefix mode the result is the first trip or the **release constraints** of the exempt hits — the hit and its look-ahead, or the quotation through its closing delimiter. The ordinary mode is byte-for-byte `v0.1.10`'s: every existing outlet and restatement test passes unchanged. The look-ahead (40), the quotation scan's reach (three K), the lag (K plus the look-ahead), the window floor's reach (the restatement's look-behind plus six K), and the hold's bound (six K, the hit, three K, the lag) are named constants with their derivations; `judge_message` is split into the body-reading part and `judge_rendered`, which the stream shares.
- **The stream check and the hook** (`StreamCheck`, `Filter.stream`; [07] items 7 and 10, `docs/research/owui-stream-hook.md`): the inlet stashes the user's normalized date forms and confirmation flag under one key of `__metadata__` in a `dict` subclass whose `repr` and `str` name lengths and flags and never a character of text, because the pinned frontend formats the whole request into a DEBUG log line once per completion call. The hook, synchronous and inline in the frontend's event loop, recognises the Chat Completions chunk (one choice, a delta with `reasoning`, `reasoning_content`, `thinking`, or `content`, a `finish_reason`), passes the usage chunk, the `selected_model_id` announcement, and an `error` event through, and fails closed on any other shape. Each text field feeds a bounded check that judges the window from a floor derived from `since` (about eight K of text, 0.3 ms against 13 ms for a whole 50,000-character thinking), releases up to the lag point, and **never splits an exempt hit from the context that exempts it**: release freezes at the larger of the released length and the constraint's start, retracts nothing, and resumes once the look-ahead or the closing delimiter has passed the lag point. The first non-empty answer delta finishes the reasoning and flushes its tail in the same chunk (the frontend appends the reasoning, closes the item, then appends the content; only a non-empty `content` closes it); the finish chunk flushes every tail; a trip replaces the chunk's text with the refusal — after a paragraph break when answer text was released — and every later chunk keeps its structure with its text removed, an unrecognised one dropped. The hook's own exception is the refusal under `guardrail/error@1`: the browser path drops a raising hook's chunk and keeps streaming, the API path tears the whole response down.
- **The outlet's two duties** (`Filter.outlet`): with the stream's state on `__metadata__`, a tripped stream is replaced whole without re-judging and its trip passed to `record_trip`; a clean stream that ended without a finish chunk has its held tails appended to `content`, the message item's text, and the reasoning item's text (created before the message item if absent) before the whole is judged; a finished stream is judged as before; the state is deleted in a `finally`, since the frontend spreads the metadata into the post-answer task calls. The malformed-body pre-checks stay outside the fail-closed guard, so its `except` never runs with an unbound name. A Stop from the composer never runs the outlet, so a stopped answer loses its held tail, at most the lag.
- **Three lag bounds** (each derived from K; ADR-0017 `exempt: derived`): `LAG_CHARS` 190 ordinarily; twice that while an exempt restatement waits for its look-ahead; `QUOTE_HOLD_CHARS` 1,690 while a quotation hold stands in the thinking. Measured on the box at the engine's median 198 characters per second under two concurrent runs: about 1.0 s, 1.9 s, and 8.5 s (0.7, 1.5, and 6.5 s at ticket 35's 260).
- **The harness flipped** (`tools/turns/run.py`, `tools/turns/classify.py`; ADR-0023): `STREAM_LEAK_FAILS` is true — a released-prefix leak on the raw route and a flash from a seat fail their rows. `stream_verdict` and the browser mode's live judge judge every released prefix through the Filter's ordinary judge over its floor with the decided length (reset when a painted text does not extend the last), which the hook's release rule makes exact frame by frame; the eight-K `_reasoning_window` is gone. The classifier reads any stored content equal to the refusal as `replaced (in the stream)` unless an `originalContent` that does not end in the refusal names the outlet's own trip — a stream trip before any answer text leaves the content equal to the refusal already, and the pinned frontend keeps `originalContent` only when the outlet changed the content.
