## Changes

- **The reasoning withheld** (`compose/open-webui/functions/arithmetic_guardrail.py`, version 5; the plan's Solution Architecture §§1–3): the stream hook builds no `StreamCheck` for the reasoning field — every reasoning delta (`reasoning`, `reasoning_content`, `thinking`) leaves the hook empty, except the turn's first, which leaves as `REASONING_PLACEHOLDER`, one space, because the pinned frontend opens its reasoning item for a truthy delta alone (`utils/middleware.py:5196` at v0.11.3) and absorbs an empty one without a trace, so a seat sees "Thinking…" then "Thought for N seconds" with an empty body instead of the pulsing cursor for the whole thinking phase (the claim's finding: the frontend keeps no block for an empty stream; the maintainer ruled the timer). The outlet empties every reasoning item's text and any `reasoning`/`reasoning_content` string on the message unless the item carries the placeholder or nothing, `rendered_texts` returns the answer's texts alone as plain strings, `judge_text`/`judge_rendered`/`_is_restatement` lose their `thinking` parameter, and the unfinished-stream flush appends the content tail alone. Gone with the judgement they served: the quotation hold and its provisional constraints, the introduced-question exemption, `QUOTE_REACH_CHARS`, `QUOTE_HOLD_CHARS`, and the state's `reasoning` entry (`placeholder_sent` in its place; a stash of the old shape reads as none, the stricter side). The look-behind `_is_restatement` reads is now derived: `RESTATEMENT_REACH_CHARS` = the longest refusal prefix (from the `REFUSAL_NEGATIONS` and `REFUSAL_VERBS` phrase tables, which the regex alternatives are built from too) + the family gap + the from-phrase's bound, `EXEMPTION_REACH_CHARS` equal to it; the lag for the answer is unchanged.
- **The answer-side restatements** (the plan's §§4–5; the ticket's ruling (c)): `normalized_dates` maps every date form the family recognises to one `YYYY-MM-DD` key (a month name or abbreviation with or without its period, `D Month YYYY`, `M/D/YY(YY)` with a two-digit year in this century, ISO as it stands — a mapping, no arithmetic), so the third quoted form — `from the Jan. 12, 2026 cert denial` against a prompt's "January 12, 2026" — is the user's date in every form; the claim found it tripping on the surface comparison, not its shape. Three constructions over a supplied date: the question form `whether … <date> [noun words] was timely` (the bounded clause after `whether`, or after `if` under a refusal prefix alone — a bare `if` is a conditional as often and governs the date on both sides), `FROM_DATE_FORM` (a refusal verb's object reaching `from the|your|a|that|this [possessive words] <date> <noun phrase>` within the family gap, a coordinated verb allowed, a clause break not), and the refusal's that-clause naming the date as the deadline (`can't confirm that June 5, 2027 is the deadline` — the one reverse shape a refusal governs, `THAT_CLAUSE` anchored at the verb's object: a direct `that`, or noun words and a coordinated refusal verb, never a new subject). The no-clause-break shape keeps a hyphen joining letters (`one-year`, `final-judgment`) and breaks on a spaced hyphen or a dash; a noun word begins and ends with a word character. The bypasses the code review found are unit cases: a noun tail after a bare `if`, a clause-break word or a spaced dash in a possessive slot, a `that` after a new subject, the adversative `but`, the affirmative twin of each form, an unsupplied date in each. `PATTERN_SET_VERSION` stays 1: ticket 09's rule bumps it when a pattern changes, and no pattern regex moved (the claim's finding (3)).
