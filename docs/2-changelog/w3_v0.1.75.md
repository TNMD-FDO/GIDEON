## Changes

This release implements slice-1 ticket 45, under the plan `docs/1-plans/guidelines-level-and-point-totals.plan.md`. Before it, the Guidelines family caught a months range the model resolved. It missed a total that stopped short of a range, so a wrong total offense level or criminal history category could reach the screen. The user would then take that figure to the Sentencing Table.

- **Two patterns** join the Guidelines family (`compose/open-webui/functions/arithmetic_guardrail.py`) as its fourth and fifth. Both answer with the unchanged Guidelines refusal, in the stream and at the outlet.
  - `guidelines/level-total@1` catches a total, adjusted, final, combined, or resulting offense level. The level must be resolved by one of:
    - a link word or an arrow;
    - a clause-final "for a total of N" with a level figure in reach;
    - a placing verb ("puts him at level 21").
  - `guidelines/points-to-category@1` catches a criminal history category resolved from a point count, in either order.
  - What still passes: a threshold ("16 or greater", "less than level 14"), a guideline's "add 3 points", the table's own definition (a list of counts is excluded), a base offense level, and a count followed by its noun.
- **The figure reader widens.** A user's figures may now be a range, a pair (keyed with its two components), a level, a category, or a point count. A second scan keys a chained total's result. As a result, a user's generic "for a total of 21" supplies no level. The confirmation context still reads only a range or a pair.
- **The attribution construction.** This is `Family.attribution_form`, used by the Guidelines and sentence-credit families.
  - **What passes:** a figure credited to a court, a PSR, probation, the government, or the Bureau by a finding verb placed right before it.
  - **How the check works:**
    - The check is end-anchored at the pattern's `anchor` group, or at the match's start when the pattern has none.
    - It reaches back no further than `ATTRIBUTION_REACH_CHARS`, which is at most `EXEMPTION_REACH_CHARS`.
    - It runs before the supplied-figure test. A hit rejects the match.
  - **The tense rule:** an actor takes the past tense, and a document takes the past or the present.
  - **What still trips:** a modal, a present-tense actor, a new sentence, a coordinated second verb, and a comma.
  - **Controls freed:** the Guidelines seed's `case-01`, and the sentence-credit seed's `attribution-01` and `case-credit-01`.
  - **Deadline family:** unchanged, by the maintainer's ruling. The form would also free its `control-39`; this is noted on ticket 44.
- **The seed.** `eval/seed/guardrails/guidelines-range.yaml` moves to set version 2. It was appended to and now holds 45 positives and 42 controls. Every committed verdict is unchanged except the three freed controls. The Function's frontmatter version is now 9, and the three render fixtures were regenerated.
- **Tests.** `tests/test_arithmetic_guardrail.py` adds the widened Guidelines cases and a new `Attribution` class. The literal-verdict test moves the three freed controls to no trip. The gate passed: 319 tests and 3,321 subtests.
