## Changes

This release implements slice-1 ticket 85 under the plan `docs/1-plans/concurrent-overlap-test-races.plan.md`. The turn harness's overlap test asserts that some session's chat finder chose its own chat among three candidates (`max(candidates) == 3`). The fake `Frontend` that serves it only guaranteed that the three chats were created together, never that a later listing still saw them: after the completion barrier's release, a fast peer could list, read, and delete its chat before a clean-snapshot session's listing was computed, and that session counted two. Hosted CI's `checks` job saw it on `v0.2.3`'s release commit and after `v0.1.71` (`AssertionError: 2 != 3`). The harness itself was never at fault.

- **The fake's second rendezvous** (`tests/test_turns.py`, `Frontend`): `barrier=N` now holds N sessions in lockstep on two rendezvous. The existing one follows each managed completion; the new one follows each post-turn listing, which is computed under the fake's lock as before and then held, outside the lock, until all N sessions' post-turn listings are computed. A managed completion that takes the completion rendezvous marks its thread; the next listing on that thread is the post-turn one and clears the mark. The sign-ins', pre-turn, and cleanup listings are unmarked and take no rendezvous. The overlap test and every other test stand as committed; a fake built without `barrier=` behaves as before.
- **The lesson** (`docs/4-unit-tests/TESTING.md`, the threaded-fake bullet): a fake's rendezvous follows the read it guarantees, and a widening is chosen for the race at hand and first shown red over the unfixed code.
- **The proof** (`.scratch/slice-1/assets/85-widen-runs.txt`): `85-widen.py late-clean 60` was 0 of 60 green over the fake as committed, then 60 of 60 over the fixed fake, with `baseline 600` 600 of 600 and ten of ten rounds of the eight `-k concurrent` tests green.
