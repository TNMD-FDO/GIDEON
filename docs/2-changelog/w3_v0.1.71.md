## Changes

The arithmetic guardrail's deadline family gains a fourth bounded pattern, `deadline/days-elapsed@1`. It catches a day count the model produced from a matter's dates and stated in the elapsed voice, and replaces the answer with the unchanged deadline refusal, in-stream and at the outlet (slice-1 ticket 44; spec §16, §18.2–18.3; ADR-0006, ADR-0017). The plan is `docs/1-plans/deadline-elapsed-forms.plan.md`.

- **The pattern** (`compose/open-webui/functions/arithmetic_guardrail.py`, Function version 8):
  - Five bounded alternatives: a count followed by an elapsed verb in the past or perfect ("200 days of the one-year period had elapsed"); a count left after one ("leaving 165 days"); a count used of the period; a count resolved from two dates, in either order ("From March 2, 2026 to June 5, 2026 is 95 days"); and a period that ran for a count ("the clock ran for 120 days").
  - Tense and the noun form draw the line. A rule's period in the present ("the clock runs for 365 days") and the adjectival period ("the 90-day period has run") pass.
  - The pattern is appended fourth in `DEADLINE_FAMILY.patterns`, so the three shipped patterns keep every committed verdict. The elapsed vocabularies are release constants and starting values (ADR-0017).
  - After review, the perfect and passive auxiliaries are separate, so "200 days of the period were used" trips and "were been used" does not.
- **The handoff**: `ELAPSED_EXCLUSION_SOURCE` rejects a count joined to a credit noun and a count placed on a sentence. The sentence-credit family then judges the credit-noun count as `sentence-credit/credit-count@1`, and the sentence-owned count trips nothing.
- **The supplied-figure reader** widens to dates and day counts. `normalized_deadline_figures` returns the date keys plus `<n>-day` keys, with number words and compounds mapped to digits. The four shared constructions are rebuilt over `DEADLINE_FIGURE`. A family-own `whether`-led construction, `DEADLINE_COUNT_QUESTION_FORM`, fills `Family.echo_form`. `reverse_form` stays the date form. The user's count restated under a governing construction is exempt. A clause break, a bare "if", or an affirmation still trips.
- **The seed** (`eval/seed/guardrails/deadline-trap.yaml`): the header moves to `pattern_set_version: 2`, and cases are appended, never edited. It now holds 39 active positives (the `elapsed-` shapes, `indirect-04`, `buried-03`, and the `bypass-` twins) and 63 active controls. The case description and the illustrative count in the elapsed voice are held as controls within the one-in-twenty ceiling. The README's deadline line follows.
- **The tests** (`tests/test_arithmetic_guardrail.py`): a new `Elapsed` class; the `SentenceCredit` handoff enumerated with the measured verdicts; new adversarial bound texts; the `Seed`, `Stream`, and `TripRecording` classes covering the new positives by construction.
