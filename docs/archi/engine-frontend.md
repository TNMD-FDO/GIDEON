# Engine and frontend — architecture leaf

**Read this when** implementing `engine verify`, the frontend client and the managed turn, the model records, the engine service and the frontend's connection to it, the two Filters (the arithmetic guardrail, the citation stamp), and the branch gate; the owning modules are `gideon/host/engine.py`, `gideon/host/enginesample.py`, `gideon/host/owui.py`, `gideon/host/owuiturn.py`, `gideon/guardrail.py`, `compose/open-webui/functions/{arithmetic_guardrail,citation_stamp,branch_gate}.py`, and their tests; the render modules that emit the engine service, the connection, and the records are [`render-apply.md`](render-apply.md)'s; the map is [`../ARCHI.md`](../ARCHI.md); its §8 table names every command's leaf.

## Commands

- **The frontend client** (`owui.py`): `Client` is `http.client` over the ingress on loopback — the site CA, SNI **and** `Host` set to the site hostname (Caddy routes by both), bearer auth, every failure an `OwuiError` with a fix, credentials excluded from every repr and message; `Client.stream` yields SSE payloads until the required `[DONE]` under a monotonic deadline; `bootstrap` is the idempotent desired-state push apply and the contract test share; a plain-HTTP base URL serves the CI stack and the drill. `grafana.Client` mirrors it over the shared `ingress.SniHTTPSConnection`. **The managed turn** (`owuiturn.py`, the recipe of `docs/research/owui-chat-routes-machine-caller.md`, the one home the turn harness, `engine verify`, and the search-sentinel contract share): sign-in for a session token, which no endpoint allowlist binds; the browser's new-chat form with the two ids the caller mints and no `session_id` or `background_tasks`, so the frontend runs the whole path once; the turn's own chat identified by `find_turn_chat` among those new since it — every candidate read, the one whose `history.messages` holds both minted ids the match, a candidate answering the route's not-found status skipped as another session's deleted since the listing, none or several a refusal with its fix — read back from `chat.history.messages` under the finished-and-error-free rules, and that chat alone deleted, deletion requiring the frontend's boolean (slice-1 ticket 68).
- **The two Filters** (§16, §15, ADR-0006; `arithmetic_guardrail.py`, `citation_stamp.py`; `docs/research/owui-stream-hook.md` for the pinned frontend's facts): stdlib-only — the trip writer's lazy, guarded import of the pinned image's Postgres driver the one exception — with no valves, toggle, or requirements, each rendered verbatim as a manifest Function and compiled in `render/owui.py` before any sync, so a broken edit refuses. **The guardrail's judge** — everything below but the inlet, the stream hook's chunk handling, the outlet, the metadata state, and the task cancel — is `gideon/guardrail.py` (ADR-0045 (b)), standard library at module level, which `engine verify` and the turn harness import; the Function cannot import the package and keeps a copy of the same statements, `tests/test_guardrail_equality.py` failing on any difference until ticket 09 deletes the copy with the Function. The guardrail's families are judged in `FAMILIES` order — the deadline family (its fourth pattern a day count in the elapsed voice, slice-1 ticket 44), then the Guidelines family (slice-1 ticket 42; its fourth and fifth patterns a resolved total offense level and a category resolved from a point count, ticket 45), then the sentence-credit family (slice-1 ticket 70) — each a `Family` carrying its refusal, its figure form and normaliser (the figures a user may supply, keyed canonically: for the deadline family a date or a day count; for the Guidelines family a months range, a level-and-category pair with its two components, a level, a category, a point count, or a chained total's result; for the sentence-credit family a date, a month-and-year or season form of its own, and a day, month, or year count with number words and compounds mapped to digits), the four restatement constructions built over that form, an optional fifth of the family's own (the sentence-credit family's echo: a determiner-led supplied count joined to a credit noun; the deadline family's `whether`-led supplied count), an optional sixth, the **attribution construction** (on the Guidelines and sentence-credit families, ticket 45: an actor subject with a past-tense finding verb, or a document subject in the past or present, reaching the pattern's `anchor` group — else the match's start — with no clause break, end-anchored over `ATTRIBUTION_REACH_CHARS`, a hit rejecting the match before the figure test as an exclusion's does; a modal, a present-tense actor, a new sentence, or a comma trips), its confirmation context (the Guidelines family's still over a range or a pair), and its own pattern-set version; a trip is replaced by the tripped family's refusal, the error path and a stateless stream by the deadline's, and `REFUSALS` is the one export the tools read, from the module. A `Pattern` may carry an **exclusion** — a bounded regex searched around the match whose hit covering the match's start rejects it, so an earlier family hands a shape to a later one (the deadline's expire, last-day, and credit-shaped day-count shapes, and its elapsed pattern's credit-noun and sentence-owned counts, to the sentence-credit family) or a pattern refuses its own (a cap word before a count), a hit deeper inside a match rejecting nothing — and a `yields_to` list, the families whose matched confirmation context skips it; a family's confirmation context may **displace** an earlier family's for the message (the sentence-credit family's displaces the deadline's, so a release-date ask is its own). Rates are excluded by a look-ahead after the count's unit or the credit noun, markdown bold admitted around the rate word. The inlet stashes each family's supplied figures and the families whose confirmation context matched, the branch (the request's model id), and the source (`eval` for the eval identity, else `user`) on the request's metadata, its state's `repr` content-free, and holds the session gate, a transport-only gate (a `user`-role request with neither a session id nor a chat id), admins and the eval identity passing. **The branch gate** (`branch_gate.py`, ADR-0045 (d), general-turn ticket 04) is a Function of its own, inlet-only with no valves, toggle, state, or message read: a `user`-role request whose model entry — the frontend's `__model__` parameter — carries no `info.base_model_id` is refused, an unusable `__user__` too, admins and the eval identity passing. Both raise before the engine with a fixed refusal; the pinned frontend runs global inlets in `(priority, id)` order and neither Function has valves, so the guardrail's inlet runs first and a raise ends the chain — the two refusals never both answer (`docs/research/owui-filter-function.md` §3.3). The page's title task after a refused first message is the recorded residual (slice-1 ticket 43). Its stream hook recognises the Chat Completions chunk alone, withholds every reasoning delta — the turn's first leaves as one fixed space, so the pinned frontend opens its "Thinking…" timer over an empty body, the rest empty (slice-1 ticket 37) — judges the `content` delta over a bounded window, releases everything but the lag, and **never splits an exempt hit from the context that exempts it**; a trip records its row, replaces the chunk's text with the refusal, and discards every later chunk's text; on a request whose metadata carries the frontend's task id — a browser turn — the first later chunk still generating requests the turn's own task cancellation, the frontend's Stop path, and is dropped, so the engine's connection closes and the stored and painted message is the released prefix, the separator, and the refusal (slice-1 ticket 52); a finish-only or usage-only chunk, the direct branch, and no running task never cancel; the cost this removes, measured from a seat: the engine goes idle within a second of the cancel and the row's `ended at` minus `refused at` read 0.017 s and 0.000 s, against `v0.1.18`'s 269.82 s for a turn whose refusal showed near 25 s; the finish chunk flushes the tail; the hook's own exception is the refusal. Its outlet empties any reasoning text the message carries, judges the answer alone, replaces a tripped stream whole without recording again, records a trip of its own judgement, appends the content tail of an upstream that closed without a finish chunk, and deletes the state; a Stop from the composer never runs the outlet, and on the browser path a stream trip ends the answer at the refusal and the outlet does not run. The restatement rule exempts a match on the user's own figures (dates canonical across written forms; a range or a pair to one key) under a bounded construction alone — the module's comment names the four and their bypasses — with a derived look-behind; a bare opening affirmation is the first family in order whose confirmation context matched, and the Guidelines family's in-text confirmation shape runs only under its context (a figure plus an ask), where the deadline family's is unconditional, because Guidelines doctrine opens with "Yes" beside "category". **The trip's row** (`guardrail_trips`, [`stack.md`](stack.md); ADR-0027, ADR-0039): `record_trip` builds the content-free row — branch, family, pattern id, source — and a daemon thread inserts it as the audit role, the password read from the frontend's secret mount at write time; every failure is dropped unlogged, so the hook never waits and the refusal never depends on the record. The stamp's outlet appends its label (ADR-0043) once, in the content and the message item, under a citation-shaped answer, and stamps on its own internal error.
- **`engine verify`** (§6.7, §19.4; `engine.py` over `enginesample.py`): `preconditions` (root; the no-GPU marker, which prints one `engine … skipped` row and exits 0 before any other read, writing no kept event; the site file, the rendered Compose, the profile, the engine healthy now, the sample, the eval identity's password held in memory, the audit writer's probe) → `needle-32k` → `needle-128k` → `needle-256k` → `structured` → `smoke` → `frontend-<id>` per sample case → `audit`, each row printed as its check completes and handed to the caller's `observe` seam, **every check running after a failure**, exit 0 iff every row is ok. The engine is reached by `docker compose exec` into its own container, where a fixed script streams the key from the secret mount into a private curl configuration and posts the body from stdin, so the key never enters the host process, an argument, a row, or a repr; a failed request shows curl's diagnostic, never a body. Two pieces of that path are public and shared with [`eval.md`](eval.md)'s judge, which is the module's only other caller: the **engine-target resolution** — the rendered Compose naming the engine, `models.lock`'s profile and generator pin, the served name and window, the engine healthy now — returning the target or a `Problem` whose texts are `engine verify`'s own, and the **reply-values reader** over a completion body. The curl script, its marker, and the call itself are unchanged by that sharing, and this command's rows and texts are its test module's contract. The needle is sized exactly against the engine's own tokenizer and judged by an exact substring test; the structured case validates the reply in code over the keyword subset the loader admits, a violation naming a key, an index, or a marker, never text; the smoke reports the single-stream rate and the two lags it implies from the judge's own bounds — figures, never a threshold (ADR-0017). The frontend-path checks sign in once as the eval identity (a failure fails every frontend row and sends nothing), make one managed turn per case on General's preset, classify the chat read back record-wide — `leak` (the module's `judge_message` trips on the stored record), `computed` (a new calendar date over the answer's texts, by its canonical `normalized_dates`), `refused-stream`, `outlet-replaced`, else `answered` — and delete the chat the finder identified in a `finally`, a chat not provably the command's own left alone; a positive passes on `refused-stream`, `answered`, or `computed` (the last recorded, never failed) and fails on `leak` or `outlet-replaced`; the trip passes on `refused-stream` alone. Every row carries figures, never text. The kept event is one `audit_log` row of kind `engine_verify`: outcome, profile, served model, window, the sample's sha256, and per check its figures. The sample (`eval/engine-verify/`) is release content read from the checkout; its README records which cases join in slices 2 and 3, why a positive cannot demand the refusal, and that a case is superseded, never edited.
