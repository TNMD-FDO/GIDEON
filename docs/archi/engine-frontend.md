## Commands

- **The frontend client** (`owui.py`): `Client` is `http.client` over the ingress on loopback — the site CA, SNI **and** `Host` set to the site hostname (Caddy routes by both), bearer auth, every failure an `OwuiError` with a fix, credentials excluded from every repr and message; `Client.stream` yields SSE payloads until the required `[DONE]` under a monotonic deadline; `bootstrap` is the idempotent desired-state push apply and the contract test share; a plain-HTTP base URL serves the CI stack and the drill. `grafana.Client` mirrors it over the shared `ingress.SniHTTPSConnection`. **The managed turn** (`owuiturn.py`, the recipe of `docs/research/owui-chat-routes-machine-caller.md`, the one home the turn harness, `engine verify`, and the search-sentinel contract share): sign-in for a session token, which no endpoint allowlist binds; the browser's new-chat form with the two ids the caller mints and no `session_id` or `background_tasks`, so the frontend runs the whole path once; the turn's own chat identified by `find_turn_chat` among those new since it — every candidate read, the one whose `history.messages` holds both minted ids the match, a candidate answering the route's not-found status skipped as another session's deleted since the listing, none or several a refusal with its fix — read back from `chat.history.messages` under the finished-and-error-free rules, and that chat alone deleted, deletion requiring the frontend's boolean (slice-1 ticket 68).
- **The guardrail's judge** (§16, ADR-0006, ADR-0045 (b); `gideon/guardrail/`, its five modules — `grammar.py`, `families.py`, `judge.py`, `writer.py`, and `window.py` — importing downward in that order): standard library at module level and the judge's one home since general-turn ticket 09 — General's API service (`gideon/api/judged.py`, [`api.md`](api.md)) over every completion it serves, `engine verify`, and the turn harness import it, and none of them edits it. The guardrail's families are judged in `FAMILIES` order — the deadline family (its fourth pattern a day count in the elapsed voice, slice-1 ticket 44), then the Guidelines family (slice-1 ticket 42; its fourth and fifth patterns a resolved total offense level and a category resolved from a point count, ticket 45), then the sentence-credit family (slice-1 ticket 70) — each a `Family` carrying its refusal, its figure form and normaliser (the figures a user may supply, keyed canonically: for the deadline family a date or a day count; for the Guidelines family a months range, a level-and-category pair with its two components, a level, a category, a point count, or a chained total's result; for the sentence-credit family a date, a month-and-year or season form of its own, and a day, month, or year count with number words and compounds mapped to digits), the four restatement constructions built over that form, an optional fifth of the family's own (the sentence-credit family's echo: a determiner-led supplied count joined to a credit noun; the deadline family's `whether`-led supplied count), an optional sixth, the **attribution construction** (on the Guidelines and sentence-credit families, ticket 45: an actor subject with a past-tense finding verb, or a document subject in the past or present, reaching the pattern's `anchor` group — else the match's start — with no clause break, end-anchored over `ATTRIBUTION_REACH_CHARS`, a hit rejecting the match before the figure test as an exclusion's does; a modal, a present-tense actor, a new sentence, or a comma trips), its confirmation context (the Guidelines family's still over a range or a pair), and its own pattern-set version; a trip is answered with the tripped family's refusal, the error path and a stateless stream with the deadline's, and `REFUSALS` is the one export the tools read, from the module. A `Pattern` may carry an **exclusion** — a bounded regex searched around the match whose hit covering the match's start rejects it, so an earlier family hands a shape to a later one (the deadline's expire, last-day, and credit-shaped day-count shapes, and its elapsed pattern's credit-noun and sentence-owned counts, to the sentence-credit family) or a pattern refuses its own (a cap word before a count), a hit deeper inside a match rejecting nothing — and a `yields_to` list, the families whose matched confirmation context skips it; a family's confirmation context may **displace** an earlier family's for the message (the sentence-credit family's displaces the deadline's, so a release-date ask is its own). Rates are excluded by a look-ahead after the count's unit or the credit noun, markdown bold admitted around the rate word, whose positive twin, each such pattern's **rate form** (`Pattern.rate_form`, compiled from the same source), is prefix mode's release constraint and never judged for a trip. The restatement rule exempts a match on the user's own figures (dates canonical across written forms; a range or a pair to one key) under a bounded construction alone — the module's comment names the four and their bypasses — with a derived look-behind; a bare opening affirmation is the first family in order whose confirmation context matched, and the Guidelines family's in-text confirmation shape runs only under its context (a figure plus an ask), where the deadline family's is unconditional, because Guidelines doctrine opens with “Yes” beside “category”. **The lag window** (`StreamCheck`, `StreamState`: text in, released text out) holds the answer's trailing span back until it has been judged and **never splits an exempt or rejected hit from the context that decides it** — a restatement's look-ahead, an exclusion hit reaching past the match, a rate phrase after a count (general-turn ticket 12); which caller feeds it, what withholds the reasoning, what a trip does to the engine's connection, and the one stored shape a trip leaves — the released prefix, the module's separator, the refusal — are the service's ([`api.md`](api.md)). **The trip's row** (`guardrail_trips`, [`stack.md`](stack.md); ADR-0027, ADR-0039 superseded by ADR-0045 at general-turn ticket 09): `record_trip` builds the content-free row — branch, family, pattern id, source — and a daemon thread inserts it as the audit role, the password read at write time from `gideon-api`'s own secret mount, the service its one consumer since general-turn ticket 07; every failure is dropped unlogged, so the caller never waits and the refusal never depends on the record.
- **The branch gate** (`branch_gate.py`, §16, ADR-0043, ADR-0045 (d), general-turn tickets 04 and 09): the one GIDEON Function inside the frontend and its only global inlet since the cutover — stdlib-only, rendered verbatim as a manifest Function and compiled in `render/owui.py` before any sync so a broken edit refuses, with no valves, toggle, state, or message read. A `user`-role request whose model entry — the frontend's `__model__` parameter — carries no `info.base_model_id` is refused, an unusable `__user__` too, admins and the eval identity passing; it raises before anything is called, with a fixed refusal, and being the only inlet there is no ordering left to hold (`docs/research/owui-filter-function.md` §3.3). It stays because the wire cannot tell the hidden base row from General ([`../frontend-contract.md`](../frontend-contract.md) row 4). The page's title task after a refused first message is the recorded residual (slice-1 ticket 43).
- **`engine verify`** (§6.7, §19.4; `engine.py` over `enginesample.py`): `preconditions` (root; the no-GPU marker, which prints one `engine … skipped` row and exits 0 before any other read, writing no kept event; the site file, the rendered Compose, the profile, the engine healthy now, the sample, the eval identity's password held in memory, the audit writer's probe) → `needle-32k` → `needle-128k` → `needle-256k` → `structured` → `smoke` → `frontend-<id>` per sample case → `audit`, each row printed as its check completes and handed to the caller's `observe` seam, **every check running after a failure**, exit 0 iff every row is ok. The engine is reached by `docker compose exec` into its own container, where a fixed script streams the key from the secret mount into a private curl configuration and posts the body from stdin, so the key never enters the host process, an argument, a row, or a repr; a failed request shows curl's diagnostic, never a body. Two pieces of that path are public and shared with [`eval-slices.md`](eval-slices.md)'s judge, which is the module's only other caller: the **engine-target resolution** — the rendered Compose naming the engine, `models.lock`'s profile and generator pin, the served name and window, the engine healthy now — returning the target or a `Problem` whose texts are `engine verify`'s own, and the **reply-values reader** over a completion body. The curl script, its marker, and the call itself are unchanged by that sharing, and this command's rows and texts are its test module's contract. The needle is sized exactly against the engine's own tokenizer and judged by an exact substring test; the structured case validates the reply in code over the keyword subset the loader admits, a violation naming a key, an index, or a marker, never text; the smoke reports the single-stream rate and the two lags it implies from the judge's own bounds — figures, never a threshold (ADR-0017). The frontend-path checks sign in once as the eval identity (a failure fails every frontend row and sends nothing), make one managed turn per case on General's preset, classify the chat read back record-wide over the service's one stored shape — `leak` (the module's `judge_message` trips on the stored record), `computed` (a new calendar date over the answer's texts, by its canonical `normalized_dates`), `refused-stream` (the record ends in a family's refusal), else `answered` — and delete the chat the finder identified in a `finally`, a chat not provably the command's own left alone; a positive passes on `refused-stream`, `answered`, or `computed` (the last recorded, never failed) and fails on `leak`; the trip passes on `refused-stream` alone. Every row carries figures, never text. The kept event is one `audit_log` row of kind `engine_verify`: outcome, profile, served model, window, the sample's sha256, and per check its figures. The sample (`eval/engine-verify/`) is release content read from the checkout; its README records which cases join in slices 2 and 3, why a positive cannot demand the refusal, and that a case is superseded, never edited.
