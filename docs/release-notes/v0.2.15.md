# GIDEON v0.2.15

Template: 2

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. This release is plumbing: GIDEON's own service learned to pass a question through to the model and hand the answer back as it is written, word by word rather than all at once, and to stop the model the moment you press Stop instead of letting it finish into nothing. Nothing your questions travel through has moved — the chat still reaches the model directly — and the new path is used only by GIDEON's tests and its own checks on the box. It is the road the checked, cited answer will travel later.

## What was bumped

No pin moved since v0.2.14.

## Coverage

General only; the Legal chat arrives at go-live on SCOTUS and the Sixth Circuit.

## Next maintenance window

No maintenance window is announced. The box has no users until go-live, when that tag's note announces the first window.

## Rules of use

Nothing from General goes into a court filing unchecked, and until go-live there is no Legal chat to check it in.
