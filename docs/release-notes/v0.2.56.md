# GIDEON v0.2.56

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. When you give an answer a thumbs
down, a person at your office can now read that question and answer once a
month, on the server itself, and turn it into a new test question in their own
words. That is how a disliked answer can lead to a better one. Your ratings and
your chats stay on the office's server and are not copied anywhere else.
