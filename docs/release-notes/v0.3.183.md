# GIDEON v0.3.183

Template: 3

## What is new

General now answers questions about the law's own figures: how long an appeal
period runs, the good-conduct credit rate, a Guidelines level, or how a rule
counts days. It still refuses to work out a date, a day count, a range, or a
credit from the facts of a matter, including a date you give it, and it still
does no sums or percentages on your figures.

Chats now expire on their own. Each night the box deletes every chat left idle
longer than the office's retention period, together with the files attached to
it and the feedback given on it, and records each deletion in the audit log.

Behind the scenes, the database has more working memory for its larger checks,
and a check that runs out of it now names the right fix. The Corpus board shows
durations as a clock (hours, minutes, seconds) and its columns under plain
names. Two checks used only in testing now run correctly.
