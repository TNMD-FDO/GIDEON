# GIDEON v0.3.68

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. The release carries the first
corpus lockfile: the list of the legal corpus files GIDEON will search once the
Legal chat arrives, each pinned by its size and fingerprint. It covers the
Supreme Court and the Sixth Circuit from the September 30, 2026 case-law
release. The maintainer writes a lockfile with one command on the server; an
office never runs it and never edits the file. The search itself is not yet
built from it. Upgrading to this release briefly restarts GIDEON's chat service,
its background worker, and its download gateway.

## What was bumped

No pin moved since v0.3.67.

## Coverage

General only; the Legal chat arrives at go-live on SCOTUS and the Sixth Circuit.
