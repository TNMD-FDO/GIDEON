# GIDEON v0.3.124

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing you see in the chat has changed. When you send a General message with
web search turned on, GIDEON now keeps a short record that a searched turn
happened: who sent it, in which chat, and when. The record never holds what you
typed or what was searched for, and a message sent without search leaves no
record.

## What was bumped

No pin moved since v0.3.123.

## Coverage

General only; the Legal chat arrives at go-live on SCOTUS and the Sixth Circuit.

tag's note announces the first window.
