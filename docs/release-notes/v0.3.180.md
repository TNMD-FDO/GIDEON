# GIDEON v0.3.180

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing user-visible in the chat. Behind the scenes, the case-law reader now
recognises more of how an opinion is laid out: a separate or dissenting opinion
opens at its own line instead of running on under the court's opinion, and
introductory material printed after an opinion's text is no longer taken for its
heading. Old versions of the search index are now cleared away a week after a
newer one replaces them. The attorneys' grading packets for the search-quality
review are built and ready to send.

## What was bumped

No pin moved since v0.3.179.

## Coverage

General only; the Legal chat arrives at go-live on SCOTUS and the Sixth Circuit.
