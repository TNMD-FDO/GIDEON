## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. When GIDEON's guardrail stops an answer, it keeps a small counting record of the fact — which guardrail, and whether the turn came from a person or from the developers' own testing. That record is kept without any of the question, the answer, or who asked. One part of GIDEON had been failing to keep it at all; this release fixes that, so the safety statistics are complete.
