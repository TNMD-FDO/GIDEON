# GIDEON v0.3.98

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. In the observability runbook, the
table of alerts now lists every alert under the exact name the alert email's
subject, `gideon status`, and the Overview's *Needs attention* list print, so
the row to follow is the one under that name. The runbook and the *Needs
attention* panel also say that a silenced alert stays listed there until its
condition clears: a silence stops the email, and `gideon status` marks the alert
silenced and leaves it out of its count. Installing this release restarts the
monitoring dashboards and three background services briefly; the chat model is
untouched.
