# GIDEON v0.2.43

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. The box gains `gideon status`,
a read-only command for the person who looks after the box. It shows on one
screen whether anything needs attention today, what is waiting on a person's
decision, and the box's vital facts: the version, services, backups,
certificate, and disk space. It writes nothing and changes nothing. The chat,
its answers, and its coverage do not change.

## What was bumped

No pin moved since v0.2.42.

## Coverage

General only; the Legal chat arrives at go-live on SCOTUS and the Sixth Circuit.

## Next maintenance window

No maintenance window is announced. The box has no users until go-live, when that
tag's note announces the first window.
