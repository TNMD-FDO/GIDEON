# GIDEON v0.3.87

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. Every Saturday morning GIDEON sends
the office one email to show that its alerting still works. That email used to look
like an urgent alert, with "FIRING" in its subject and a line asking for action. It
now has its own subject, "Channel heartbeat", and two plain lines saying the alerting
is alive and nothing is owed. It no longer appears in the box's list of things that
need attention. A Saturday without this email is still the sign that something is
wrong, and the start-here card now says what to check first. Upgrading to this
release restarts the monitoring dashboards and the chat's back end for about a
minute.
