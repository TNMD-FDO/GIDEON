# GIDEON v0.2.0

Template: 2

## What is new

General is GIDEON's first chat: a general assistant that can search the web when you turn search on and confirm the notice. It verifies nothing, and when an answer carries a citation it adds the line "General does not verify citations."

General's instructions are a default, not a guarantee: a system prompt you set yourself under Settings → General is added after General's, never in its place.

An answer that computes or confirms a filing deadline is held back while it streams and replaced with GIDEON's fixed refusal before the date is shown. A request sent to GIDEON by hand, outside the chat window, is refused with "GIDEON answers only through its chat window. Open a chat and ask there."

If General is listed but a turn never answers, the engine behind it is down and the CSAs have already been alerted, within about six minutes; wait or try again later rather than reporting a broken model list. Most restarts take one to three minutes and alert no one.
