# GIDEON v0.2.59

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. When GIDEON refuses to work out a
deadline, a sentencing range, or a sentence credit, it already kept a short
record of the refusal. That record now also keeps the chat's id, never its words
or who asked. Once a month a person looks at the refusals that got a thumbs-down
in the same chat. A thumbs-down on a refusal is how you tell us it was wrong, and
a wrong refusal becomes a new test case, so it is less likely to happen again.
