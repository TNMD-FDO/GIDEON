# GIDEON v0.3.42

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. GIDEON now runs a background
worker: the service that will fetch, read, and index the court opinions behind
the Legal chat, one job at a time, outside any chat turn. It has no work yet
beyond a test job; the corpus jobs arrive later in this slice. A job cut off by
a restart runs again on its own. Installing it restarts only the service a chat
turn passes through and the monitoring page, as an upgrade does; nobody is
signed out.
