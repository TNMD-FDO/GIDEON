# GIDEON v0.3.4

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing you can see in the chat has changed. For the CSA who watches the box,
Grafana now opens on the Overview board. Its top row lists the alerts that need
attention right now and links the other boards and the start-here card. Every
board links back to the others, and each card has a short description of what
it shows and what to do when it reads badly. Every alert email now lists each
alert with its summary and runbook section and ends with one line saying what to
do first. The backup-age, drill, and driver-version cards now show the right
values, and the cards over the box's records, which stayed empty in the browser,
now draw.

## What was bumped
