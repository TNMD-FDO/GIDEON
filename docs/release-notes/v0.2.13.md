# GIDEON v0.2.13

Template: 2

## What is new

Nothing in GIDEON itself has changed — no command, no screen, and nothing you type is treated differently. This release is housekeeping on the public copy of the source: the files that only matter while GIDEON is being built no longer travel with it, the front page now points only at documents you can actually open, and the issue forms say plainly that a bug or an idea is answered on the issue and that contributing is by invitation.

## What was bumped

No pin moved since v0.2.12.

## Coverage

General only; the Legal chat arrives at go-live on SCOTUS and the Sixth Circuit.

## Next maintenance window

No maintenance window is announced. The box has no users until go-live, when that tag's note announces the first window.

## Rules of use

Nothing from General goes into a court filing unchecked, and until go-live there is no Legal chat to check it in.
