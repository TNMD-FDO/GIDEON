# GIDEON v0.3.161

Template: 3

## What is new

Nothing user-visible. Installing a case-law release now reads the official
list of Supreme Court decisions the Court has overruled, matches each listed
case to the opinions GIDEON holds, and keeps every listed pair, matched or not,
with counts of what matched. The Legal chat will use it to warn that a cited
case was overruled; that warning arrives with the Legal chat at go-live.

## What was bumped

No pin moved since v0.3.160.

## Coverage

General only; the Legal chat arrives at go-live on SCOTUS and the Sixth Circuit.

No maintenance window is announced. The box has no users until go-live, when that
tag's note announces the first window.
